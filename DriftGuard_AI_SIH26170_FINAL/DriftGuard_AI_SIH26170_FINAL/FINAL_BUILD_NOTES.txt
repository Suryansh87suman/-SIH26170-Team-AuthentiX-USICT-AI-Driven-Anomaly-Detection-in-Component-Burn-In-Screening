2. Double-click RUN_DASHBOARD_WINDOWS.bat.
3. The launcher upgrades dependencies, verifies/rebuilds models if required, then starts Streamlit.

If a model-loading error ever appears after changing Python/sklearn:
- Double-click REBUILD_MODELS_WINDOWS.bat once.
